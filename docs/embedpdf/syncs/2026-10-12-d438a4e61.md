  diff. `CFX_FileBufferArchive` → `FileBufferArchive` (`1efc5bb8d`).

  generator's header forward-declares it the same way as upstream's `blend.h`.

  its neighbour's content stream, so `GenerateContent()` writes it in place.

- **New API:** `FPDFPageObj_AddExistingMark()`, `FPDFTextObj_SetFontSize()`.
