- **Axial shadings (`2f8880782`)** step the gradient coordinate once per pixel instead of
  transforming each pixel: 4-6x faster on gradient-heavy pages. The exact math moves some gradient
  pixels slightly; upstream updated its corpus goldens (`shade_expected*.png`, via the
  `pdfium_tests` roll). Engine render expectations with axial shadings may move the same way when
  the engine adopts this runtime.

- **AGG clip masks (`4a72657f7`)** intersect row by row, 40% less render CPU on upstream's test

  `cpdf_read_validator.h`, `cfx_face.cpp` and `cfx_fontmapper.cpp`.

  `cfx_agg_cliprgn.cpp` are taken as they are.
