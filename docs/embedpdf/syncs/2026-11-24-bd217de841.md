## Upstream changes outside the conflicts

- **FreeType 2.14.3-146 to 2.15.0-10 (`0b43db186f`, `0d0cf3a356`, `96cb92b773`, 16 commits):**
  bounds the `hdmx` advance lookup (`023fd06e3`) and fixes overflows in CFF blend (`79d80cadf`) and
  variable composite axis values (`e2526d30e`), among others. PDFium's custom config still turns
  variable fonts off (`TT_CONFIG_OPTION_GX_VAR_SUPPORT`), so its `avar` version 2 change does not
  reach us. No new golden differences.
- **Text page overflow (`721a620a0a`):** `CPDF_TextPage::FindTextlineFlowOrientation()` checks page
  size before casting to `int` and saturates the line height. Upstream's two new tests pass.
- **Destruction observers (`0fb188cc6c`, `b47c2e370e`):** `CPDF_DataAvail` now watches a document
  through `CPDF_Document::DestructionNotifier`; `ObservedPtr` no longer needs a virtual base class.
  Nothing of ours implemented the old `Observable::ObserverIface`.
- **Form-fill JS call-outs (`66ed17ef8f`):** every remaining `IPDF_JSPLATFORM` call-out sets
  `in_active_ffi_callback_`, which makes destroying the form-fill environment from inside one a
  CHECK. The engine never drives the form-fill environment.
- **Rendering (`8a07c5de59`, `cb73733715`, `4c92ae872b`):** Highway versions of `MultiplyAlpha()`
  and `MultiplyAlphaMask()` for BGRA, and BGRA-to-mask compositing in 16-bit lanes; upstream's tests
  check them against the scalar loops.
- **`HashBitmap()` removed (`afcec981f6`):** `EmbedderTest::BitmapsEqual()` replaces it. Our tests
  compare renders of documents that close in between, so a digest suits them; a copy of the helper
  now lives in `fpdfsdk/epdf_edit_test_util.{h,cpp}`, and our 42 uses are unchanged.
  `epdf_save_embeddertest.cpp` and `epdf_layer_embeddertest.cpp` include it now.
- **Drawing tests (`2bc1255c48`, `aff95024b4`, `873ded4e41`):** a new runner,
  `run_drawing_tests.py`, compares `pdfium_test --dump` output with expected text. It takes over
  tests from the embedder suite (`LoadError547706`, `RenderAnnotWithOnlyRolloverAP`,
  `RenderPageToDisplayList` and the whole of `fpdf_parser_decode_embeddertest.cpp`), and our CI does
  not run it. See Tests and Proposed, not done.
- **Build:** `PDF_ENABLE_FONTATIONS` and `PDF_ENABLE_FREETYPE` move to the public config
  (`6372d801de`), `pdfium_common_config` loses its empty `cflags_cc` and `ldflags` lists
  (`bd217de841`) and gains `target_gen_dir`. None of our additions used the removed lists.
- Not in our builds: XFA and V8 (`c9a1ea6d05`, `20e2d231bb`, `dd810f17f5`), Skia and Fontations
  (`30f1c04da5`, `171c17bfd4`, `b5232dabe0`), XFA's barcode and text-out tests (`c6a3053418`,
  `86e81c6e7c`), fuzzers (`5f70cd3483`).
