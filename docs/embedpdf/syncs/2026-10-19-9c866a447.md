## Upstream changes outside the conflicts

- **Display-list tests.** `e804c9576` (annotation), `02aeb0078` (parser decode), `f14ea7f3d` (page
  import), `c3e1143e8` (flatten) and `067393bda` (`DeletePageAndRender`) compare display lists
  instead of PNGs. Six of our Mac pixel failures go away with them.
- **Layer-mode harness (rule layer-mode).** The display-list renderer draws the form through the
  form handle (`GetOrCreatePageView()`), and the form-fill page view isn't layer-safe. `RenderPageWithFlags()`
  already skips `FPDF_FFLDraw` in layer mode; `RenderPageWithFlagsToDisplayList()` now drops the
  handle the same way. Without it, the flatten tests `Bug889099`, `Bug890322` and `Bug896366` hit
  the layer's write assertions in layer mode instead of failing as expected.
- **XFA FormCalc (`c9a34bc1d`, `a50f1a595`).** The context's destructor clears the binding of its
  value, and a date-symbols loop drops an `UNSAFE_TODO`. Not in our builds (`pdf_enable_xfa=false`).
- **JPX `sycc_to_rgb()` (`9c866a447`)** uses float literals; same results.
- **lcms (`bb72e525b`)** removes a stale patch file; no code change.
